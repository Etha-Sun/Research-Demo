Semantics: NEON vmaxq_f32 propagates NaN; x86 _mm*_max_ps returns the SECOND operand when an operand is NaN (here vm127);
       the scalar fallback a > b ? a : b also returns b.                                                   [S1]

       non-NaN inputs the output is bit-for-bit unchanged (x86 exhaustive). Performance not measured.         [S1]
